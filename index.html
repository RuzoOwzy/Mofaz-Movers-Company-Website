About Mofaz Movers

A dynamic Tanzanian freight forwarder built on flexibility and integrity.

Mofaz Movers Logistic Co Ltd is a dynamic freight forwarding company driven by an unwavering belief in its founding ideology: the flexibility, speed and innovative spirit of an ever-changing team, combined with the strength, security and integrity of a major clearing and forwarding, shipping and logistics service provider.

With years of experience in sea and air freight logistics, Mofaz Movers is known for offering reliable and efficient freight forwarding services throughout the world. We are dedicated to handling your cargo and to ensuring an expeditious clearance process. With Mofaz Movers, punctuality is guaranteed by our staunchness in your cargo logistics – from clearance to delivery.

How a consignment moves

From enquiry to delivery, in five steps

The same route every consignment takes through our office – so you always know where yours is and what happens next.

  1. 1

    Enquiry and quotation

    Tell us the cargo, the route and the timeline. We come back with a costed plan and the compliance requirements up front.

  2. 2

    Booking and documentation

    We book the space with the line or airline and assemble every document customs will ask for, before the vessel arrives.

  3. 3

    Customs clearance

    Declaration, assessment and release – managed by agents who deal with Tanzanian customs every working day.

  4. 4

    Transport and warehousing

    Bonded or general storage where you need it, and inland transport with us as the responsible carrier.

  5. 5

    Delivery and hand-over

    Cargo delivered to your door, with the full documentary trail handed back to you.
