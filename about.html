Overview

Who we are

Mofaz Movers Logistic Co Ltd is a preeminent logistics provider and an icon among Tanzanian freight forwarders, offering a wide range of services including international freight clearing and forwarding, shipping, customs clearance and all other freight forwarding solutions. We consolidate cargo of every kind across East Africa and the world, and we provide customs clearing and forwarding, bonded warehousing and storage, supply chain, freight shipping and transportation services.

Although we have a wide variety of services to offer, our most important tool is our personalised service. At Mofaz Movers, customer service has been reinvented as the cutting edge of competitiveness in the clearing, forwarding and logistics industry. Championed by a well-established worldwide network – from shipping lines to prominent freight forwarders and clearing agents – Mofaz Movers is a reputed name in both the domestic and the international market.

We enjoy strong relationships with our business partners, airlines, shipping lines and other cargo handlers, enabling smooth, secure and prompt delivery of your cargo. There is no better time than now to give us a try.

Mission & vision

What we are working towards

Our vision

To remain Tanzania’s preferred clearing, forwarding and logistics company – applying insight, service quality and innovation to create sustainable growth for business within Tanzania and internationally.

Our mission

To provide our clients with an effortless experience for all their import and export needs, without compromising on quality, efficiency or integrity. Through our highly skilled staff complement we deliver a cost-effective customs clearing service within Tanzania.

Core values

Teamwork and Professionalism

We hold ourselves to the highest ethical standards on every consignment we handle.

Compliance
