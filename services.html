Service 01

Clearing and Forwarding

Customs clearance for air and sea freight, handled end to end between shipper, client and customs.

  • Arranging customs clearance for both air freight and sea freight consignments.
  • Meeting the documentary requirements that sit between the shipper, the client and customs management.
  • Managing declarations and duty assessment so consignments move without avoidable delay.
  • Proactive follow-up to keep cargo clear of storage and demurrage charges.

Service 02

Importation of Vehicles and Machines

Sourcing, shipping and clearing vehicles and heavy machinery from anywhere in the world.

  • Buying vehicles and machines on your behalf from source markets worldwide.
  • Arranging shipment, inspection and all documentation for the unit.
  • Handling the full clearing process on arrival, as one package.
  • Advising on duty, age restrictions and compliance before you commit to a purchase.
Enquire about this service

Service 03

Exportation

Export of Tanzanian produce and goods, from sourcing at origin to discharge at the final port.

  • Buying local products on request – cashew nuts, sesame, avocados and other agricultural produce.
  • Arranging all export documentation and regulatory permits.
  • Finding vessels and booking space to the final port of discharge in the specified country.
  • Coordinating consolidation and pre-shipment handling.
Enquire about this service

Service 04

Bonded Warehousing, Storage and Transportation

Bonded and general storage in bulk, plus inland transport as the principal carrier.

  • Temporary storage of products in bulk before shipping, or individually to end consumers.
  • Consultation on the most efficient and cost-effective means of transport for your goods.
  • Transport on normal terms or under contract, acting as principal transport operator responsible for the carriage of goods.
  • Nationwide delivery from the port of Dar es Salaam to the destination.

Service 05

Logistics and Supply Chain Solutions

Turning your supply chain from a cost centre into a competitive advantage.

  • Dependable, efficient delivery mechanisms in a world where things change in an instant.
  • Visibility and reporting across the movement of your consignments.

Our process

How your consignment moves through our office

  1. 01

    Enquiry and quotation

    Tell us the cargo, the route and the timeline. We come back with a costed plan and the compliance requirements up front.

  2. 02

    Booking and documentation

    We book the space with the line or airline and assemble every document customs will ask for, before the vessel arrives.

  3. 03

    Customs clearance

    Declaration, assessment and release – managed by agents who deal with Tanzanian customs every working day.

  4. 04

    Transport and warehousing

    Bonded or general storage where you need it, and inland transport with us as the responsible carrier.

  5. 05

    Delivery and hand-over

    Cargo delivered to your door, with the full documentary trail handed back to you.
